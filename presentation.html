The Challenge

$33B

Annual global credit card fraud losses

0.173%

Fraud rate — 1 in every 578 transactions

Why is this hard?

  • Severe class imbalance — naive "all-legitimate" model scores 99.8% accuracy yet catches zero fraud
  • Dual cost problem — false positives freeze customer cards; false negatives miss real fraud
  • Hidden features — raw transaction data anonymised via PCA; V1–V28 have no direct business interpretation
  • Research goal — can ensemble methods + SMOTE overcome the imbalance problem?

Limitations & Future Work

Current Limitations

  • 2013 dataset — modern fraud patterns differ; model may not generalise to card-not-present or contactless fraud
  • Opaque features — V1–V28 are PCA-transformed; can't derive actionable business rules from model weights
  • Static model — no online learning; fraud rings evolve and cause concept drift over months
  • Threshold fixed — default 0.5 probability threshold; business cost ratio (false positive vs. false negative) not tuned
  • No temporal validation — random 80/20 split; time-based split would be more realistic

Future Directions

  • Deep learning — LSTM / Transformer on raw transaction sequences to capture temporal spending behaviour
  • Cost-sensitive threshold — tune the classification threshold using real bank cost ratios for missed fraud vs. frozen cards
  • SHAP explainability — provide auditable explanations per-decision for regulatory compliance (EU AI Act)
  • Online learning — incremental updates to adapt to new fraud patterns without full retraining
  • Graph neural networks — model merchant/card relationships for ring-fraud detection

Conclusions

01
Ensemble Methods Win
Random Forest F1 = 0.8022 vs. Logistic Regression F1 = 0.1015. Ensemble captures complex non-linear fraud patterns that linear models miss.
02
Random Forest → Production
Best precision (83.9%) + strong recall (76.8%). Only 14 false alarms protects customer experience. Recommended for deployment.
03
XGBoost → Max Recall
Catches more fraud (82.1% recall) but 57 false alarms. Choose when missing fraud is more costly than customer inconvenience.
04
SMOTE is Essential
Applied only to training data. All 3 models achieve >75% recall. Without balancing, models default to predicting "all legitimate".
